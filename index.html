PeakLog Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 26, 2026

PeakLog stores your list progress, completion status, favorites, notes, and selected photos so core features work as expected.

PeakLog uses Apple CloudKit to sync your data across your own devices when iCloud is enabled.

Location is only used to show your position on the map if you enable that setting.

Photo Library access is only used when you choose to save photos to your library.

Contact: support@yourdomain.com
